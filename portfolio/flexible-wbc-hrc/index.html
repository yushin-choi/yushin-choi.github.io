    Flexible Whole-Body Control Framework for Human-Robot Collaboration

    Graduate Researcher · Mar. 2023 – Feb. 2025
    Hanyang University · HRC Lab

    Overview

    This project developed a unified whole-body control framework for tasks that move between human–robot coexistence and physical collaboration. Instead of switching abruptly between independent controllers, the framework continuously adjusts task priorities according to interaction risk.

    Risk-aware obstacle avoidance on the mobile-manipulator platform.

    Method

    Human–robot risk variables derived from relative motion and interaction context continuously modulated task sensitivities inside a dynamically consistent generalized hierarchical controller. This allowed obstacle avoidance, nominal task execution, and physical guidance to transition within one control hierarchy.

    Contributions

    • Developed a hierarchical whole-body controller with risk-driven continuous task-priority transitions
    • Integrated human-centric risk variables into the control hierarchy to modulate robot responsiveness
    • Evaluated tunable safety–comfort–efficiency trade-offs in blended coexistence and collaboration scenarios

    Evaluation

    The joint study evaluated multiple sensitivity settings across a blended sequence of human approach, collision avoidance, and physical collaboration. The settings exposed the expected trade-offs among safety, operator comfort, and task efficiency without requiring abrupt controller switching.
