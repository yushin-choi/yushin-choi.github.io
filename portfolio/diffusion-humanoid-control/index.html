    Guided Diffusion for Humanoid Whole-Body Control

    Research Engineer · Jan. 2025 – Present
    LIG Defense & Aerospace

    Overview

    This ongoing exploratory project studies cost-guided motion generation from a learned humanoid motion prior. The goal is to preserve coherent whole-body motion while steering the model toward task objectives.

    Approach

    • Implemented a cost-guided latent diffusion model for humanoid motion synthesis
    • Combined a learned motion prior with task-specific costs applied during generation
    • Evaluating guidance behavior for motion objectives beyond dominant training modes

    Preliminary Preview

    The following early simulation preview shows task-guided whole-body motion generated from the learned prior. The method and evaluation are still under development.
