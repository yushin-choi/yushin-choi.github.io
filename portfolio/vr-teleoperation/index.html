    Viewpoint-Aware VR Teleoperation with Force Feedback

    Graduate Researcher · Mar. 2023 – Feb. 2025
    Hanyang University · HRC Lab

    Overview

    This project studied teleoperation in which the operator’s viewpoint changes independently of the remote robot. The control architecture transformed motion and force information between the operator, virtual environment, and robot frames to preserve intuitive interaction.

    Physical operator setup and the corresponding Unity-rendered workspace.

    System

    The physical platform combined a Summit XLS mobile base, a Franka Research 3 manipulator, an ATI AXIA80 force/torque sensor, and a Meta Quest 2 controller. A six-axis virtual gantry robot in Unity mapped head-dependent motion commands into the remote workspace, while ROS and TCP/IP relayed robot state and contact-force information between the physical and virtual systems.

    Contributions

    • Developed viewpoint-aware pose and force transformations for VR teleoperation
    • Implemented inverse kinematics for a Unity-based virtual gantry robot
    • Relayed simulation-generated contact forces through a physical leader manipulator to provide force feedback

    Demo

    Joint project demonstration from the Hanyang Human-Robot Collaboration Research Laboratory.

    Outcome

    The reported target-reaching experiments showed that viewpoint-aware control reduced both end-effector travel and task-completion time relative to a fixed-view baseline. The work was presented at the Korea Robotics Society Annual Conference in 2024.
