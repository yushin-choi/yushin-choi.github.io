    Whole-Body Control for Balancing a Quadruped Mobile Manipulator

    Graduate Researcher · Mar. 2023 – Feb. 2025
    Hanyang University · HRC Lab

    This project addresses balance control for a quadruped mobile manipulator while its arm moves a passively connected object whose dynamic effects are not known to the controller. The robot must track the manipulation trajectory while compensating for the resulting whole-body disturbance.

    Paper PDF DOI Video

    Approach

    I developed a hierarchical whole-body controller that regulates both linear and angular centroidal momentum while coordinating balance, end-effector tracking, and posture objectives. The controller does not require a predefined dynamic model of the passively connected object.

    • Formulated momentum, end-effector, and posture objectives in a unified task hierarchy
    • Integrated linear and angular centroidal momentum regulation with manipulation tracking
    • Implemented and validated the controller using the RaiSim physics engine

    Experiment

    The robot tracked a three-dimensional arm trajectory on a 15° slope while the center of mass of the connected object changed during motion. I compared three controller variants: no momentum regulation, linear-momentum regulation only, and regulation of both linear and angular momentum.

    Results

    In the representative case with the object mass set to 0.5 kg, the no-momentum and linear-momentum-only controllers lost balance. Regulating both linear and angular momentum maintained balance while the robot continued tracking the manipulation task.

    Publication

    This work resulted in my first-author journal article in The Journal of Korea Robotics Society.
