    About

    I am currently a Research Engineer in Robotics and Payload Control Systems at LIG Defense & Aerospace.

    I received my M.S. in Intelligence and Information Engineering from Hanyang University in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Wansoo Kim. My thesis focused on hierarchical whole-body control for improving the adaptability of mobile manipulators. I received my B.S. in Robotics from Hanyang University ERICA in 2023.

    My research aims to enable adaptive humanoid loco-manipulation by integrating whole-body control with learning-based motion generation, particularly through generative models.

    Research Interests

    • Humanoid Whole-Body Control — dynamically feasible control of high-dimensional, contact-rich systems
    • Generative Motion Models — diffusion models and learned motion priors for controllable motion synthesis
    • Learning-Based Loco-Manipulation — combining learned planning with model-based whole-body control

    Selected Research

    Guided Diffusion for Humanoid Whole-Body Control

    Ongoing exploratory work on cost-guided latent diffusion for controllable humanoid whole-body motion.

    Whole-Body Control for Quadruped Balancing

    Centroidal-momentum regulation for balance under unknown manipulation disturbances.

    Flexible Whole-Body Control for Human-Robot Collaboration

    Risk-driven continuous task-priority transitions across coexistence and physical collaboration.
