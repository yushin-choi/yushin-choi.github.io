    Reinforcement Learning-Based Whole-Body Control for Dynamic Manipulation of Passively Connected Objects with a Legged-Arm Robot

    Published in The Korea Robotics Society Annual Conference (KRoC), 2025

    Summary

    This conference paper investigates a hybrid architecture in which an RL agent generates a swing-up trajectory in a reduced-order task model and a whole-body controller tracks that trajectory on a legged-arm robot.

    Contributions

    • RL-based trajectory generation for dynamic manipulation
    • Integration with whole-body tracking on a quadruped mobile manipulator
    • Analysis of the feasibility gap between low-dimensional task trajectories and full-robot execution

    Recommended citation: J. P. Jang, S. H. Hwang, Y. S. Choi, S. W. Hwang, and W. S. Kim, "Reinforcement Learning-Based Whole-Body Control for Dynamic Manipulation of Passively Connected Objects with a Legged-Arm Robot," in The Korea Robotics Society Annual Conference (KRoC), 2025.
