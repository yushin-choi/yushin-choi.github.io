    Whole-Body Control for Balancing an Articulated Legged-Arm Robot with the Integration of Momentum Management and a Passively Connected Unknown Object

    Published in The Journal of Korea Robotics Society, 2025

    Summary

    This paper develops a hierarchical whole-body controller for a quadruped mobile manipulator affected by a passively connected object with unknown dynamic properties. Centroidal momentum regulation is incorporated into the control hierarchy to improve balance while the robot tracks manipulation objectives.

    Contributions

    • Hierarchical whole-body control with centroidal momentum regulation
    • Unified balance and manipulation objectives for an articulated legged-arm system
    • Physics-simulation validation under unknown object disturbances on a 15° slope
    • Comparison with linear-momentum-only and no-momentum baselines
