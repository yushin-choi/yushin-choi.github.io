    Whole-Body Control of an Articulated Legged-Arm Robot for Maintaining Balance with a Passively Connected Unknown Object

    Published in The Korean Society for Precision Engineering (KSPE), 2024

    Summary

    This work studies whole-body balance control for an articulated legged-arm robot interacting with a passively connected object whose dynamic properties are unknown. It served as an early conference presentation of the momentum-aware balance-control research later developed into a journal article.

    Contributions

    • Whole-body control formulation for coupled balance and manipulation objectives
    • Treatment of disturbance effects from a passively connected object
    • Simulation-based controller evaluation

    Recommended citation: Y. S. Choi, S. H. Hwang, and W. S. Kim, "Whole-Body Control of an Articulated Legged-Arm Robot for Maintaining Balance with a Passively Connected Unknown Object," in The Korean Society for Precision Engineering (KSPE), 2024.
